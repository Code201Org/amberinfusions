A little clarity

Before you begin.

A few things to know about your care.

How do I choose an infusion?+
All infusions require a consultation and prescription. Your clinician reviews your health history and individual needs before recommending an option.
How does a programme differ from one session?+
A kit contains the components for one session. A programme is a prescribed course of sessions over several weeks, with the appropriate kit chosen for each session.
How long should I allow for a session?+
Infusion time is typically around 60–90 minutes, depending on the prescribed formulation. Allow additional time for your consultation and preparation, and confirm the total visit time with your clinician.
What should I discuss with my clinician?+
Share your health history, current medicines and supplements, any allergies, and any pregnancy or breastfeeding considerations. Ask about suitability, possible risks, alternatives and what to expect from your session.
